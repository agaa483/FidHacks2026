Quick facts

Credit scores range from 300 to 850. Above 670 is considered good. Above 740 is great.

Credit history does not transfer across countries. Even if your family had great credit back home, you start at zero in the US.

The single most important thing you can do is pay your bill on time every month. That alone is 35% of your score.

Starting credit at 18 instead of 25 makes a significant difference by the time you need an apartment or a car loan.
